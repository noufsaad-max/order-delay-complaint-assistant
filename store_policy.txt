Oasis Market (a fictional online store)
Policy for replying to delayed-order complaints

1. Greet the customer and apologize for the delay.
2. Never promise a specific delivery date unless the shipping team has confirmed it.
3. Never show the full order number. Show only the last two digits, like ***45.
4. Tell the customer that the support team will follow up on the shipment and send an update within 24 business hours.
5. If the delay is more than 7 business days, the customer may cancel the order for a full refund.
6. If the customer did not give an order number, politely ask for it.
7. If the customer sounds upset, acknowledge their frustration with empathy.
8. Keep the reply short and polite (2 to 4 sentences).
